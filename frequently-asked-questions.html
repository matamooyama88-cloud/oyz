Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find immediate answers to questions about orders, shipping within South Africa, materials, ring sizes, and custom engraving.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does delivery take in South Africa?
Standard courier delivery takes 3 to 5 business days nationwide. For bespoke, photo-engraved, or custom name necklaces, please allow an additional 2 to 4 working days for careful craftsmanship before dispatch.
How much is shipping?
We offer Free Courier Delivery on all orders over R800. For orders under R800, a standard flat-rate delivery fee of R99 applies across all South African provinces.
Can I track my order once dispatched?
Yes! As soon as your jewelry is packaged and dispatched, you will receive an email and SMS with a direct tracking link to monitor your delivery in real-time.

Materials & Sizing

What materials does GNOCE use?
GNOCE pieces are crafted from 925 Sterling Silver, 18k Yellow Gold Vermeil, and Rose Gold blends, accented with AAA Cubic Zirconia crystals and hand-painted enamel. All pieces are nickel-free, lead-free, and hypoallergenic.
How do I determine my ring size?
You can wrap a thin strip of paper or string around your chosen finger, mark where it overlaps, and measure the length in millimeters to find the circumference. Match this measurement with our standard SA/UK ring size chart or contact customer care for guidance.
Can I customize or engrave a necklace or charm?
Yes! We offer photo projection necklaces, engraved birthstone charms, and personalized name necklaces. Simply upload your high-resolution photo or chosen text during checkout.

Returns & Customer Care

What is your returns policy?
We offer a hassle-free 60-Day Return Guarantee on all standard jewelry pieces in unworn, original condition with packaging. Personalized items with custom photo engravings are backed by our quality guarantee.
How should I care for my GNOCE jewelry?
Store your jewelry in the provided velvet pouch or airtight box when not in use. Avoid contact with perfumes, lotions, chlorinated pool water, and harsh chemicals. Gently polish with a microfiber cloth to keep the shine brilliant.

Still Have Questions?

Our friendly customer care team is available Monday to Friday to assist with sizing, custom orders, or order status.
